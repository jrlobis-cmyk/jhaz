A letter for you

Dear Jazlee,

I have started this letter many times, and each time I worried that my words would not be enough. I decided to write it anyway, plainly and honestly, because you deserve the truth more than you deserve a perfect speech.

First, I am sorry. I made mistakes that hurt you deeply. I damaged the trust you had in me, and the trust your family had in me too. I am not saying this to make it smaller, and I am not going to offer you excuses, because there are none that would make it okay. What I did was my responsibility. It was mine, and only mine.

When I understood how much damage I had done, I tried to fix it. But I did not always go about it the right way, and some of my attempts created even more problems. Things I did in the hope of making it better ended up hurting you again. I am sorry for that as well. I broke your trust and I caused you pain, and I did it while you were still giving me so much.

That is the part I understand most clearly now. Through everything, you kept giving me your love and your support, and I did not appreciate it the way I should have. I realized many things too late, and I wish I had seen them while they were still right in front of me. I understand now how much you mean to me, and I understand how badly I handled our relationship.

I want to be clear about what I am not asking. I cannot demand your forgiveness, and I do not believe you owe it to me. I cannot expect you to trust me right away, either. You may be scared, and you have every reason to be careful. If you are, I would never want you to hide it or push it down for my sake. Trust is not something I get to ask for. It is something I have to earn, through honesty, patience and accountability, and by being the same person day after day, whether or not anyone is watching.

I want to become better, and I want to do it for real. That means taking responsibility when I am wrong instead of defending myself, telling the truth even when it is uncomfortable, and listening when you tell me how something felt. I will not promise that I will never make another mistake, because I do not want to give you a promise I cannot be sure of keeping. What I can commit to is this: when I fall short, I will own it, and I will not leave you to carry it.

If you are open to it, I would like to ask for another chance. I am not asking because I expect the past to disappear. It will not, and I do not want us to pretend it never happened. I am asking because I hope that, if you truly want it too, we can learn from what went wrong and begin a new chapter that is healthier than the last one. I want to love you again and to feel your love again, but only if that is something you genuinely want, and only at a pace that feels safe for you.

Please take as much time as you need. Whatever you decide, I will respect it. If your answer is no, I will understand, and I will still be grateful that you took the time to read this.

Thank you for reading.
